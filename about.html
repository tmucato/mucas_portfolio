About

Engineering data people can rely on

What I do, how I work and where I spend my time.

I have spent more than ten years building data platforms, reporting and AI systems, and the software around them.

I design pipelines and warehouses, build dashboards that teams use on their own, and apply NLP and retrieval augmented generation to document heavy work. I care about the unglamorous parts: validation rules, identity matching, access control and the documentation that lets a team keep a system running after I leave.

I also lead and mentor developers and analysts, and work directly with stakeholders to turn requirements into systems that are secure, auditable and easy to run.
